Task 2 explanation

We designed a visualization for Alicia Ferrer, a road-safety technician at Barcelona City Council. She knows urban traffic and reads bar and line charts easily. She is less comfortable with complex data visualizations like heat maps and symbol maps. She works on a desktop computer, uses the dashboard monthly and must explain progress to non-technicians city managers.

Each of the six questions proposed is written in Alicia's voice and mapped to one function of the Visual Vocabulary: ranking, evolution, correlation, spatial, deviation, part of a whole magnitude. We use familiar charts first. The heat map and symbol map are less familiar, so they will need a clear legend. They are worth it because accidents depend on time and place, only a map shows where serious accidents cluster.

Two data issues affect the design. The pedestrian-cause field has a large "unknown" category (5,887 records), and it writes "not caused by the pedestrian" in two spellings. Both must be cleaned or shown explicitly.
