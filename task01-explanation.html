Task 1 explanation

Data

The dataset has 54,954 traffic accidents recorded by Barcelona's Guàrdia Urbana between 2019 and 2025. It is real recorded data, complete to 31 December 2025 and published once a year, so it needs to be yearly refreshed. Most fields are categorical (district, severity, pedestrian cause) or discrete counts with a true zero (victims, vehicles). Hour of the day is a discrete interval value with a cyclic order.

Message

For a general audience interested in road safety, the exploration shows one message: the busiest hours are not the most dangerous ones. Between 00:00 and 06:00 there are about six times fewer accidents than between 12:00 and 18:00; yet 4.2% of them are grave or fatal, against 2.2% in the afternoon. The peak is at 05.00h: 6.2% of 501 accidents, while 14h is the busiest hour 2ith 4,098 accidents and 2.2%.

Visualization

Hour is cyclic, so a radial layout is appropriate for displaying the data. The bar length encodes the number of accidents and a single-hue sequential color encodes the share of grave or fatal accidents, because the share has an order but no meaningful midpoint. We kept 24 hourly bins, pooled over seven years, and one decimal for percentages.

Caveats

Night hours have fewer accidents, their percentages are noisier. Radial bars exaggerate outer values, so counts are also shown in the labels. Blank injury counts in 2024-2025 were converted to zero, a zero by convention rather than a real one.
